Terms, privacy & disclaimer.

What you may do on the platform, what we record and why, and what the material is not. The confidentiality agreement you sign before entering a project room is the binding document for confidential material; this page governs the platform itself.

Terms of use

DealVault is operated by DealVault Operations ("the Platform") as a gated venue for presenting private projects to professional investors. By registering you confirm that the identity details you provide are accurate, that you act in a professional investment capacity, and that you are legally permitted to receive the material made available to you.

Accounts are personal. You may not share your credentials, forward platform documents to people who are not your bound professional advisers, or attempt to access projects you have not been approved for. We may suspend an account that threatens the integrity of the platform, and revoke access that the agreement entitles us to revoke.

Approval to a project's room is a discretionary decision made per project, per investor. No approval implies any other. We may decline any request without stating a reason.

The confidentiality framework

Access to confidential project material is governed by the Investor Confidentiality & Access Agreement (version DV-CONF-2026.1), which you review and sign electronically before any room opens. Its substance, in short: confidential material is used only to evaluate an investment in that project; your professional advisers may see it under equivalent duties; everything you do inside a room is recorded; documents are watermarked to you; and the obligations run for three years. The full text is shown at signing and is available on request.

The signature record — your typed name, the timestamp, your IP address at signing, and a cryptographic hash of the exact text — is stored on your account and visible to you in the account area.

Privacy

What we collect. Registration details (name, email, firm, jurisdiction, investor profile), authentication metadata (hashed password, session tokens stored only as hashes, IP address, user agent), the agreement signature record, and activity inside gated rooms (documents opened, sections viewed, reading time, downloads, prints, questions asked).

Why. To operate the gate you asked to pass through, to evidence compliance with the agreement, to give project owners a trustworthy picture of who read what, and to keep the platform safe. Activity is first-party only — there is no third-party analytics or advertising on the platform.

Sharing. Your identity and reading record for a project are visible to the Platform and to the owners of that project. We do not sell data. We disclose records where the law requires it.

Retention and your rights. Account and audit records are retained while your account is active and for three years after, matching the agreement's term. You may request an export or erasure of your personal data at any time by writing to deals@dealvault.app; erasure requests that conflict with the agreement's record-keeping obligations will be limited to the extent the law allows.

Disclaimer

Nothing on this platform is an offer to sell securities, a solicitation of an offer to buy, or investment, legal or tax advice. Briefs and documents are provided for discussion and internal evaluation only. Financial figures are illustrative planning cases prepared by the projects themselves — not forecasts, not guarantees, and not independently audited by the Platform unless explicitly stated.

Any investment decision you make will be under definitive documents agreed directly with the relevant project, and the Platform is not a party to those documents. The Platform does not verify the claims made in project materials; diligence is your job, and the audit trail is there to protect both sides while you do it.

These terms are governed by the laws of the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan, with exclusive jurisdiction in the courts of Amman, to the extent the platform itself is concerned.
