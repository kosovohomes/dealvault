The principle

A deal room has one job: get the right material in front of the right person, and be able to prove it afterwards. Browse freely. Sign once. Enter per project. Nothing unrecorded.

How it works

Four steps between you and the room.

No sales calls to get the password, no forwarded PDFs. The gate is deliberate and the record is shared with you.

  1. Step 01

    Register with a real identity

    Name, firm, jurisdiction and investor profile. Email is verified with a six-digit code before anything confidential is shown.

    Create the account
  2. Step 02

    Browse the briefs

    Every project publishes a non-confidential profile: thesis, market, stage, raise size and highlights. Express interest in the ones worth your time.

    See the showcase
  3. Step 03

    Sign the agreement once

    The confidentiality agreement covers every project on the platform: eight clauses, initialled and signed electronically, with the hash stored on your record.

    Read the agreement
  4. Step 04

    Get approved, per project

    The operator reviews each request. Approved, the project's room opens: watermarked documents, tracked downloads and prints, a diligence channel.

    Usually within one business day

Auditability

Both sides see the same record.

Confidential material is only ever served to an approved session — there is no client-side hiding. Documents carry your name, email and a per-reader watermark. Downloads and prints are logged against your access receipt, and your account page shows exactly what was recorded.

What is logged

  • Account events — registration, verification, sign-in, device and IP.
  • Requests — interest expressed, NDA signed, every approval decision.
  • Reading — documents opened, sections viewed, downloads, prints.

What is watermarked

  • Every document view carries a reader overlay: name, email, receipt.
  • Downloads are stamped with your receipt and the agreement version.
  • Redistribution is a breach of the agreement you signed.

What you control

  • See your own audit trail in the account area at any time.
  • Revoke every session instantly from the account page.
  • Ask us to export or erase your personal data.
